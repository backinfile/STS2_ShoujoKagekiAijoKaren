Fixed combat strategy:
1. Seek a safe lethal sequence before spending resources on defense. Check costs, target Block, powers, retaliation and trigger timing; numerical lethal alone is not proof.
2. Avoid death and minimize HP lost over the whole fight. Taking a small hit can be worthwhile if it prevents larger future hits by ending combat sooner.
3. Against multiple enemies, concentrate damage when removing a threat reduces future incoming damage; compare this with area damage and dangerous enemy scaling.
4. Evaluate additional damage prevented, not raw Block gained. Do not overblock unless an explicit effect rewards or preserves it.
5. Draw or generate resources before irreversible commitments when the information helps and resources remain to use it. Consider hand limits, reshuffles and harmful draw/play triggers. Apply useful modifiers before their payoffs.
6. Use quiet turns for damage or useful setup. Invest in scaling only when the fight lasts long enough to repay its cost.
7. Use available consumables when they prevent death or substantial loss; do not hoard them until too late.
8. End turn only after checking useful legal actions. Do not play a useless or harmful card merely to spend energy. Never assume unknown draws or hidden resources.
Choose one action, then reassess the updated state. Current rules and powers override these general priorities.
