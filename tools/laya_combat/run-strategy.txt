Fixed run strategy:
Improve the chance of completing the run while preserving enough health for the next threats.
Choose routes by current health, deck capability, rewards, recovery opportunities and the visible boss. Take harder fights only when their expected rewards justify the risk.
Add cards that solve current damage, defense, draw or scaling needs and work with the existing deck. Skip additions that dilute effective draws. Do not force a predefined build.
Value upgrades, removal, relics and consumables by their marginal benefit to this deck. Compare purchases against available gold and future needs.
At rest sites, compare necessary recovery against useful upgrades. In events, read the actual costs, outcomes and selection instructions; avoid unaffordable or lethal costs.
Claim beneficial rewards before leaving. Consider potion capacity and avoid wasting useful consumables.
For removal, upgrade, transform or bundle selections, satisfy the stated requirement, compare the available alternatives, and confirm a beneficial completed selection.
Use only visible information. Do not assume hidden rewards, card draws or unstated mechanics. Choose one legal action and reassess the fresh state.
