Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a new 2x2 storyboard for a polished Slay the Spire 2 card VFX, titled Unforgettable Shine. This replaces a rejected filmstrip design entirely.
Primary request: make the moment of a stage girl's brilliance unforgettable through a strong sword attack and a star that keeps shining after the stroke. Revue Starlight Karen Aijo inspired: theatrical red cape, blue-white sword light, champagne-gold starlight, a trace of coral-red. Original VFX interpretation of the childhood stage promise and the brilliance of a stage, not movie memorabilia.
Each panel shows the SAME side-on gameplay scene: a small red-caped sword girl on left, stone giant on right, dark desaturated blue stage/dungeon. Game characters about 35% panel height. Strong thick energy silhouettes with hand-painted flame/ribbon detail and soft volumetric glow; actual illustrated textures, NOT geometric line art.
Panel 1: FOCUS. A compact white-gold star ignites at the sword tip, with two short sweeping blue-white and coral-red light wisps pulling inward. A restrained overhead theatrical shaft catches the girl. Quiet anticipation, visually full but tightly concentrated.
Panel 2: CUT. A huge clean ascending crescent of icy-white sword energy sweeps from the girl to the giant's chest. Its broad body has layered blue transparent flame ribbons and warm gold flecks, a razor white leading edge, a fine red outer echo. Beautiful bold curved silhouette and strong forward motion. This is one decisive attack, no crossing multiple swords.
Panel 3: SHINE. The real hit explodes into a large asymmetrical four-point white-gold star, with sharp tapered shards and a few blue petal-shaped light fragments. The broad crescent rapidly dissolves into a small number of textured streaks. The enemy is readable through the light. No full-screen wash.
Panel 4: REMAIN. The attack is gone but one unmistakable small gold-white star still floats where it struck, surrounded by sparse dying blue-gold embers. At Shine depletion ONLY, three luminous gold stars with coral-blue comet tails curve gently back toward the girl's small real buff icon region below her. They are light, not objects or card seals. A subtle warm residual spotlight communicates that brilliance remains.
No giant circles, no filmstrips, no rectangles, no grids, no runes, no diagram lines, no floating cards or tickets, no invented crown icon. No excessive particles, no backgrounds containing text. Only the small labels 01 FOCUS / 02 CUT / 03 SHINE / 04 REMAIN. Wide composition, fine black gutters, elegant high-production 2D anime game VFX concept.
