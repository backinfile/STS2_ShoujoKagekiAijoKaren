Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production VFX storyboard for a Slay the Spire 2 character mod, one wide contact sheet with four equally sized landscape panels in a 2x2 grid.
Primary request: Design the playable card effect "星光闪耀之时" / When Starlight Shines for Karen Aijou from Revue Starlight. The card gathers the remaining uses of ONE shining card and replays that card until its shine is exhausted, then awards a future shine-card reward. Show an implementable real-time game effect, not a cinematic cutscene.
Reference motifs from research: the official Revue Starlight theme of reaching a desired star, theatrical followspots, and Hikari's licensed hair ornament with a LARGE long four-point golden star and a SMALL companion four-point star. The existing card illustration shows hands reaching toward this unequal pair against deep blue stage light.
Scene/backdrop: side-view painterly 2D card-battle arena, muted dark blue ruined theater environment; small red-caped anime Karen character at left, single plain enemy at right. Preserve a broad clear lower strip for the card hand, and enemy health bars must stay readable. Characters are scale/context only.
Four panels: 01 GATHER, two narrow soft-edged followspots converge above Karen; a large slender four-point gold-white star and a smaller companion star form beside a single floating card; faint rose-gold and pale blue trails rise toward them. 02 IGNITE, luminous gold filaments sketch a high semicircular chain of NINE small four-point lights arching from above Karen toward center stage, one real card suspended beneath the unequal double-star clasp; dramatic but transparent volumetric-looking light. 03 REPLAY, a single light in the chain is being spent, sending a graceful gold-white comet down toward the currently played card at center; the already-spent portion remains a thin fading line, unspent stars stay visible; red and ice-blue fine filament accents only, no giant laser and no duplicate cards or duplicate characters. 04 FINALE, both stars briefly flare into a tall narrow brilliant double-star shape above Karen, a compact shower of golden stardust dissolves upward and a small clean reward glint travels toward top-left UI; arena returns to normal.
Style/medium: polished game VFX concept art, very clear luminous shapes, tapered curved ribbons, controlled soft glow, painterly background, crisp four-point star silhouette, layered additive gold filaments. The same composition and scale in all panels.
Color palette: champagne gold, warm ivory, subtle scarlet/rose for Karen and pale blue for Hikari, midnight blue environment.
Text: only small restrained English panel labels "01 GATHER", "02 IGNITE", "03 REPLAY", "04 FINALE". No other typography.
Constraints: main effect fits above and near owner, with dynamic arc toward actual play position. No hard full-screen flash, no magic circles, no opaque vignette, no big geometric HUD, no massive beam, no random confetti, no copied anime frame. Draw ORIGINAL VFX composition suitable for Godot C# curves and additive drawing.
