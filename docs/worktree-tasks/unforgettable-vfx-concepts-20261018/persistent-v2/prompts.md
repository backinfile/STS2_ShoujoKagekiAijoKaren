# 常驻余痕方案：ImageGen 提示词

- 工具：内置 image_gen；三次独立新生成，未传入图像引用。
- 本轮是视觉概念，不是已实现的实机效果；角色姿势仅为示意。
- 四阶段：命中、淡化、战斗后续留存、目标消失后仍留存。四阶段的残痕属于同一击打图形，不另加纪念徽章。

## A 留光斩痕

输出：`A-lingering-cut.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual proposal storyboard for a persistent single-hit attack effect in a 2D deckbuilding game.
Primary request: Redesign Karen's card "永不遗忘的闪耀" (Unforgettable Shine). The ACTUAL IMPACT SHAPE gradually loses brightness after the hit but NEVER disappears during the current battle. A subtle physical-looking memory of that exact attack remains floating at its hit position even after the target dies. This persistence is the central new mechanic; the attack itself stays clean and simple.
Style/medium: refined hand-painted 2D anime game VFX concept sheet, restrained glow, clean decisive silhouettes, minimal elements. This is concept art, not a real screenshot.
Scene/backdrop: quiet desaturated dark blue stone combat stage with a flat side-view camera. Small anime swordswoman on the left: brown ponytail, red stage military jacket and skirt, short red cape, black boots, slim silver sword. One dark gray stone training dummy on the right. Repeat identical camera and scene in every thumbnail. Keep the stage minimally detailed and slightly brighter directly behind the impact so dim retained marks remain easy to read. No UI, damage numbers, towers, cards, other magic, extra characters, or scenery props.
Composition: wide landscape design board. Upper 62%: ONE large full-width battle-scale image showing the fresh hit. Lower 38%: FOUR equally sized, separated storyboard thumbnails from left to right, labelled exactly "HIT", "FADING", "LATER", "TARGET GONE". First thumbnail shows bright impact; second shows the exact same impact silhouette at lower brightness; third still clearly shows that same frozen silhouette much later in battle, subdued but unquestionably present; fourth removes the stone dummy entirely and STILL shows the SAME dim impact silhouette floating at the SAME empty-space location and same size. Do not replace the retained impact with a different symbol. The swordswoman remains still and small in all thumbnails.
Persistence rendering: fresh hit bright for around 0.15 seconds, fade toward a calm approximately 18-22% luminous visual strength over about 1.2 seconds, then hold that low level indefinitely during battle. The "LATER" and "TARGET GONE" marks must remain visible at identical strength: do NOT draw them empty or fully transparent. Keep exactly the same shape, angle, scale, and position across thumbnails. The retained mark has essentially no bloom, no emitting particles, no animation, no new shine pulse, no moving ribbons. Shape persistence, rather than many ornaments, communicates memory.
Constraints: one single impact and one retained shape. Modest game scale, localized at right-side target torso; maximum VFX extent about 65% of target height. Avoid circles, rings, shockwaves, explosions, showers, complex fragmented blades, rays filling the screen, text glyphs, memory photos, film strips, hearts, clocks, additional decorative stars. No global darkening, stage spotlights, screen flash, large aurora, sound or reward effect. All strike contact is at the dummy torso. Keep extensive negative space; effect is secondary to combat visibility.

Direction A — LINGERING CUT:
A single slender straight diagonal sword scar from lower-left to upper-right. Cool ivory-white core, an extremely thin ice-blue edge. Needle-tapered at both ends, length roughly 60% of dummy height, maximum width 1/28 of its length. It is ONE continuous line, no star, no X, no spark, no triangular broad wing.
The fresh hit should feel precise and decisive. The fading and later versions retain the exact straight diagonal cut as a thin desaturated silver-blue scar suspended in the air. The fourth thumbnail shows that same slash still hanging in empty space after the target vanished. No extra light point.
Text (verbatim): "A  LINGERING CUT", "HIT", "FADING", "LATER", "TARGET GONE". No other text.

```
