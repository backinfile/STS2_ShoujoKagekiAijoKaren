# ImageGen 提示词与来源

- 模式：内置 image_gen，不使用 CLI。
- 输入：新生成，未向生成工具传入图片。方向参考本项目已阅读的角色卡图、现有截图与用户历史修订约束；生成画面属于示意，非实机截图。
- 输出：三张独立方案，含主画面与三阶段缩略时间线。

## A 星痕一闪

输出：`A-star-scar.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: concept-only storyboard for a single-target attack VFX in a 2D deckbuilding game.
Primary request: Completely redesign the effect of Karen's card "永不遗忘的闪耀" (Unforgettable Shine) into something minimal, elegant, and immediately readable. This is a proposal image, not an actual game screenshot or a production sprite.
Style/medium: polished 2D hand-painted anime/game VFX development board. Deliberately simple shapes with crisp tapered silhouettes, modest bloom, sparse light. Avoid ornamental complexity.
Scene/backdrop: a quiet dark desaturated blue stone stage, stable flat side-view combat camera. A small anime stage swordswoman stands at the left (brown ponytail, red military stage jacket and short red skirt, short red cape, black boots, slim silver sword); one simple dark stone training target stands at the right. The character, target and battlefield are secondary, subdued, and identical in all frames. Do not add scenery objects, UI, card mockups, damage numbers, floating text, towers, enemies, cinematic lighting or extra characters.
Composition/framing: wide landscape concept board. Upper 70% is one large full-width combat-scale keyframe at the main strike. Lower 30% contains three small, clean, separate storyboard thumbnails showing preparation, one hit, and disappearance in order, repeating the same camera, character, and target. Short plain Latin labels for the design name and the three times only. Preserve extensive dark negative space; show the effect at realistic modest gameplay scale. Main VFX occupies at most 20% of battlefield width, localized around the right-side target; it must never fill the screen. Effect smaller than or comparable to the target torso, character and target silhouettes remain legible.
Constraints: one attack, one visual center, one simple dominant shape; at most one tiny secondary accent. No screen darkening or screen flash, no spotlights, no light cones, no circles, no rings, no shockwaves, no multi-ray explosions, no particle showers, no floating confetti, no film frames, no glyphs, no wings, no trails crossing the whole scene. No reward effect, no sound design. No large crescent blade, no layered translucent blade sheets, no fragmented inner facets, no radial burst. The lower thumbnails must demonstrate a single controlled transient effect, not simultaneous phases.

Specific direction A — STAR SCAR:
At the target, a SINGLE slender nearly straight oblique ivory-white sword cut appears from lower-left to upper-right, with a restrained ice-blue fringe. The stroke is long and needle-like, maximum width 1/35 of its length, softly tapered at both ends, clean and continuous. It is not a crescent, bolt, beam, laser, V-shape or X. Its length is about 65% of the target's height. At the hit point, embed ONE tiny pale-gold four-point star about one tenth the cut's length; its glow is very small.
Three thumbnails: "0.00" tiny glint at the sword tip, no other effect; "0.12" the single oblique fine cut at the target, with tiny gold point; "0.70" the cut is gone and only the tiny four-point star quietly fades. Large main keyframe shows the clean 0.12 strike.
Text (verbatim): "A  STAR SCAR", "0.00", "0.12", "0.70". No other text.
Mood: decisive restrained swordsmanship, a fleeting light remembered as one tiny point.

```
