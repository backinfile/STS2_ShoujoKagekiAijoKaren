## 实机预览（opaque）

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: faithful game VFX mockup painted over the provided actual game screenshot. Keep every existing game character, monster, tower, UI element, card, Chinese text, health bar and terrain exactly in its original place; keep original camera and image dimensions. Add a genuinely substantial, richly textured painted anime game effect around the brown-haired red-coated player at x330 y380. This is a rework of a rejected VFX that was only faint geometric lines: the actual runtime effect needs substantial illustrated artwork and attractive movement, not outlined circles or simplistic line art. Depict the peak of a warm 'sunset rehearsal memory' stage effect for the card 'Why are you leaving, my friend?'. Behind the player, add a large semi-transparent memory of a rehearsal-room sunset window, with two gracefully draped burgundy-red velvet curtain wings, a luminous creamy gold center and beautiful painted fabric folds, arranged in a 410 by 370 pixel localized area (roughly x100 to570, y140 to500), naturally feathered and dissolving into floating amber dust at the edges. The window is a fleeting stage-light projection, not an opaque house or UI frame. Broad, richly shaded peach/ivory/gold silk light ribbons sweep around the player's lower body in one clear protective gesture; a front strand has tactile satin highlights and wispy ends, with varying thickness 15-40 pixels, while keeping the face, sword and torso readable. At most seven small fluttering script-paper fragments with luminous edges, textured gold dust and soft layered bloom add depth; no text on the paper. Two real bottom hand cards (the leftmost two) each gain a warm silk cradle around their upper corners, a halo of drifting dust and a broad soft ribbon trailing upward toward the actual tower behind the player; the cards remain in their original positions and readable. Leave the monster untouched and the rest of the screen recognizable. Cohesive painterly game-quality illustrated VFX, detailed real fabric, translucency and volumetric light with a distinct silhouette and three clear depth planes. Strong visible warm contrast on cool blue terrain, no global white flash, no compass, no magic-circle outlines, no skinny lines, no confetti storm, no extra people, no new UI, no captions or watermark.

## 舞台素材（transparent）

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: actual transparent 2D game VFX sprite, a standalone richly painted sunset rehearsal stage memory derived from the curtain/window effect in this reference mockup. The reference is a style/composition reference ONLY. Create ONE isolated sprite on a genuinely transparent RGBA background. Include only the ephemeral red velvet curtain wings, warm sunlit rectangular rehearsal window and tiny amber dust fringes. No game screenshot, no terrain, no UI, no cards, no healthbars, no character or human silhouette, no tower, no objects or figures in front of the window, no floor furniture, no silk rings (those will be a separate sprite), no text. Canvas wide 1536x1024. Center a luminous sunset window occupying the middle 50% of the canvas, four vertical panes with slender dark antique-gold muntins; beautiful soft painted orange-pink sunset clouds and cream sunlight through it, not a literal solid wall. Two burgundy velvet curtain wings drape from upper left and upper right, swept aside with big graceful folds, fine gold fringe, richly shaded painterly cloth, a little red sheer silk behind them. Curtain wings occupy side 20% areas. Window and curtains gently disintegrate into glowing feathered amber dust at outer edges; no hard rectangular border and no opaque background around it. Bottom fades into low warm haze. Leave at least 8% fully transparent outer margin. The image must have a clear physical visual mass, fabric material detail and warm white-gold illumination as in the reference; it will appear behind a 2D anime character at 550px wide and then dissolve away over a second. Center stays relatively simple luminous panes, not a busy illustration. Refined painted anime fantasy game VFX, tangible folds, restrained soft bloom, beautiful translucent edges. The entire sprite silhouette must be fully inside frame and anti-aliased. Preserve real alpha transparency.

## 绸带素材（transparent）

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent 2D game VFX texture. Generate ONE isolated broad S-shaped glowing silk ribbon on a genuinely transparent RGBA canvas, landscape 1536x1024. This is real illustrated VFX artwork to be animated in Godot, not a diagram or a mockup. The ribbon travels diagonally from left-lower to right-upper with two graceful S turns; its overall visual bounding box is from 12% to 88% canvas width and 24% to76% height. It has substantial varying width, delicate folded satin material, several overlapping ivory/gold/peach surfaces and burgundy-rose translucent edges, beautifully painted highlights, soft internal light, thin wispy tips that disintegrate into a sparse trail of warm amber dust. Rich tactile flow as if sunset sunlight became a piece of beautiful theatrical silk. Several parallel fine fold highlights within the thick ribbon provide sheen and volume; it must look like flowing fabric/light rather than a simple curved outlined line, a tube, a lightning bolt or an inflated object. Warm white-gold luminous center, antique gold and coral edge shading. Fully isolated with spacious transparent margins, no opaque background, no floor, no window, no character, no ring, no circle, no objects, no text, no watermarks. Refined painterly anime fantasy game VFX quality. Preserve true alpha transparency and avoid a giant opaque glow rectangle.
