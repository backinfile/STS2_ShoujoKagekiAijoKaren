# Imagegen 提示词

本轮使用内置 imagegen。A–F 为独立四格分镜；D、E 在初稿上仅细化遮罩轮廓。输入与输出均用于概念预览。

## 共享生成提示词

A–C 使用以下共享提示词，再接对应方案内容。

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original game scene-transition VFX storyboard for Karen Aijo from Revue Starlight, a playable character in Slay the Spire 2.
Primary request: a rich, exciting, SHORT transition designed in the native game's silhouette-mask language. Silent has overlapping knife cuts; Ironclad has interlocking black tongues; Necrobinder has enveloping spirals. Learn their purposeful overlapping silhouettes and evolving negative spaces, and translate that richness into HARD POLYGONS, LONG STRAIGHT LINES, ACUTE TRIANGLES. Every effect contour is straight-edged. This is a graphic mask in motion.
Style: razor-clean flat 2D screen-space graphics. Opaque ink-black main masses, Karen crimson #FB5458 as selected narrow secondary planes, extremely sparse ivory edge flecks. Strong big/medium/small shape hierarchy, 8–16 purposeful irregular wedge contours. Crisp uncluttered large shapes with small fast splinters. Avoid regular triangulation grids, smooth curves, painted brushwork, illustrative objects, metallic 3D slabs, cinematic lens bloom, neon, sci-fi HUD, radial speed-line wallpaper, and ornate rings.
Composition: one landscape 2048x1152 concept board containing FOUR successive 16:9 game frames in a precise 2 by 2 layout, narrow charcoal gutters. All panels full bleed. Tiny concept letter in the top gutter, and tiny 01, 02, 03, 04 in corners only. No other lettering, slogans, timing labels or design notes.
Background continuity: first two panels have the SAME low-contrast Karen character-selection backdrop, a small secondary anime Karen with short chestnut twin tails, a small gold crown hairpin, navy stage uniform and red cape. Karen is a background hint; the TRANSITION occupies the composition. Final panel reveals a subdued beige parchment branching route map. Keep backgrounds simple, consistent, subordinate, with no invented readable UI.
Four beats: 01 first masks strike into an otherwise visible source scene; 02 bold advancing silhouettes cover about 70 percent while a deliberately designed T reads instantly; 03 completely occlude both scenes, with the SAME T contour briefly visible as a flat crimson cut/negative inset within the opaque mask; 04 large masks peel/snap away along their established straight trajectories and reveal most of the map.
Critical T: a broad HORIZONTAL CAP located ABOVE one DESCENDING CENTRAL STEM, no stem extending above the cap. Clearly an upright T, not a plus, X, or vague intersection. Make it a structural aperture or junction belonging to the moving masks, with assertive width and a long tapered stem, clearly readable in panels 02 and 03. Do not simply paste a glowing font letter on top.

```

## A · 交错剑痕

```text
Concept A. CROSSING BLADE LATTICE. Build a dramatic asymmetric network of six long, needle-tipped black triangular slashes arriving on different straight diagonals. The design feels closest to the Silent's native knife transition but has a deliberate Karen T geometry: two near-horizontal opposing blade edges form a broad top aperture at the upper third, and two steep inward cuts leave a long central descending stem. Surround it with staggered thinner triangular cuts that echo those main angles, not a starburst. 01: three elegant hairline wedges have cut far across the source, one secondary crimson counterstroke. 02: the huge interwoven black knife masses leave a complete bold T-shaped source window, with sharp triangular islands of the source outside it. 03: the same geometry locks into complete opaque coverage, T now a flat muted-crimson inlay at the cut junction, flanked by a few offset blade notches. 04: opposed diagonals split open quickly, long acute black corners and two small crimson splinters frame the revealed map. Main character: precise intersecting cuts, strongly designed asymmetry, dangerous clean rhythm.
```

## D · 棱角回旋

```text
Concept D. ANGULAR VORTEX. Reinterpret Necrobinder's enveloping spiral as THREE sweeping polygonal HOOKS, each made of 3–4 long straight segments and acute triangle tips. Entirely angular, no curved edges. Use an off-center large-scale pinwheel composition with THREE unequal arms, never fourfold symmetry. Each arm has one thin crimson offset inner facet. A clear upright T is carved into the central shared negative opening, independent of the diagonal surrounding motion. 01: long bent polygonal hooks enter sequentially from top-left, right and lower-left, wrapping toward the T center. 02: the interlocking hooks nearly envelop the screen around an instantly recognizable T-shaped source aperture, with long angular channels and triangular blade tips giving depth through overlap alone. 03: coverage completely closes, a sharp flat crimson T is visibly seated inside the black hook junction, with small broken straight crimson segments describing the former rotation. 04: angular hooks unwind to the outer corners along their straight segments, exposing the route map through an expanding polygonal opening. Main character: powerful angular circulation, richly shaped negative space, large asymmetric hooked triangles, crisp and native-mask-like.
Extra art direction: This option must feel DIFFERENT from a frontal T logo reveal. Make the 3 angular hooked masses dominate the entire frame and clearly wrap past each other in unequal jagged stepped paths, with alternating near-black and deep-crimson facets defining overlap. In panel 03, preserve a bold T-shaped junction as a cut between those large masses; fill the rest of the frame with the offset polygon hooks rather than empty black. The silhouette complexity should rival the native enveloping spiral while using only long straight segments. Do not simplify the hooks into short confetti triangles or symmetrical wings.
```
