# 抽象皇冠轮廓提示词

工具：内置 imagegen。本体能量纹理、已有战斗截图与《打击》肖像作参考；候选为完整皇冠基础轮廓，无附属装饰。

```text
Use case: ui-mockup / compositing.
Create ONE comparative concept image of THREE very simple ABSTRACT CROWN-SHAPED ENERGY TOKENS for the Karen mod in Slay the Spire 2. This revises the rejected overly ornate designs. Main requirements: a basic recognizable crown SILHOUETTE, same simplicity as the original game, clearly different from all original class energy shapes, and clear numeric overlays.
This is NOT a fancy crown ornament and NOT a circular medallion with a small crown attached. The WHOLE single token has a crown-shaped outside boundary. There must be no underlying circle, coin, diamond, star, hexagon or other backing badge.

Reference 1: actual game energy-token contact sheet. Copy its plain matte, chunky hand-painted treatment: dark outline, 2 or 3 broad red bevel facets and simple bright inner energy area. Its game classes use octagon, pentagon, eight-point sun, hexagon and square; neutral energy also uses a circle and Regent's star resource uses a four-point sparkle. Do NOT reuse any of those as the proposed outer silhouette.
Reference 2: actual combat screenshot with Karen at left, stone enemy at right, blue waterfall cave, top HUD and cards. Reuse the SAME scene in all three proposal columns. The source has '102/3'; replace the combat energy overlay with '3/3'. Any debug red character rig lines may be cleaned for presentation, but do not redesign the character or battlefield.
Reference 3: Karen Strike card portrait. Reuse this SAME supplied anime portrait within an identical complete card in all three columns.

Design language:
ONE unified red crown-shaped token, coral #FB5458, dark burgundy outline/shadows #8B2A2D and flat warm salmon inner energy well. The energy center is an unadorned broad LIGHT-RED AREA INSIDE THE CROWN BODY, not a separate gem or ring. Numerals sit across this broad body, centered safely BELOW the three top points. Tiny 2-3 curved red brushmarks may suggest energy near the inner perimeter. Render like the ACTUAL original game icons, not glossy mobile game art, not 3D, not jewelry.
No gold whatsoever. No jewels, tiny dots, filigree, rivets, ribbons, leaves, shoulder braids, epaulettes, mini-crown attached to a circle, detached base/band/underline, companion stars, sparkles, glow rays or floating particles. The crown is simply a small solid geometric/organic shape with exactly THREE top peaks. Keep total number of silhouette segments low. No logos printed inside.
The card cost must say '1'. The combat HUD and zoom must say '3/3'. The icon must work at native 74x74 for the cost and 128x128 for combat. Broad lower body is essential for the 3/3 digits.

Three subtle but visibly distinct variations of the SAME simple crown idea:
A — ANGULAR: Compact THREE-POINT CROWN as a single coarse polygon. Three upright sharp triangular top tips, center tip a little taller, shallow V valleys between. Two clean slightly inward-sloping sides, almost flat bottom with two subtly clipped corners. Broad body under points, width:height about 1.1:1. The contour alone reads as a crown; three red broad bevel facets, no distinct extra crown band.
B — SOFT: Same single THREE-POINT CROWN but with rounded top tips, softly rounded valleys, gently tapering sides and rounded lower corners. Body proportion 1.05:1. Clearly three crown peaks on the TOP and broad flat-ish bottom. NOT a flower, not a cloud, not a circle. Soft dark outline, restrained brushwork.
C — LOW & WIDE: A simple WIDE THREE-POINT CROWN, three short crisp top peaks, shallow valleys, broader lower body, squat silhouette width:height about 1.3:1. Side tips angle very slightly outward, bottom nearly flat with softened corners. The little points occupy only the top quarter; the broad salmon-red lower body fits 3/3 comfortably. Still one solid basic shape, not a wearable prop.

Output layout:
ONE 3:2 landscape comparison sheet, approximately 1536x1024 or higher. Charcoal neutral outer background. Top header 'KAREN — ABSTRACT CROWN', small 'CONCEPT MOCKUP'. THREE equal vertical columns A, B, C separated by slim rules. Keep all three columns exactly the same scale and structure. Modest typography, no long explanation.
Each column:
- Short title 'A — ANGULAR', 'B — SOFT', 'C — LOW & WIDE'.
- Upper half: one full upright Karen Strike card with red/ivory game frame, supplied identical portrait, title '打击', type '攻击', description '造成 6 点伤害。'. Its upper-left cost token is the respective SINGLE RED CROWN SILHOUETTE with digit '1', about one quarter of card width. The crown must be clearly visible at this small card scale.
- Beside the card, a SMALL blank token thumbnail (no digit) shows the unobstructed SAME crown outline. Do not put this blank token on the card or treat it as a crown on a circle.
- Middle/lower part: wide 16:9 battle viewport based on reference2. Same environment, characters and UI in all columns. Replace bottom-left energy counter with the exact SAME crown silhouette, coral red, ivory '3/3', authentic screen relative size about 6-8% viewport width. Update hand card cost tokens to the same crown silhouette at small scale. Never put a giant detached crown in the middle of the battlefield.
- At the bottom: a compact enlarged crop of the battlefield BOTTOM-LEFT HUD corner, with the same crown-shaped 3/3 energy counter and neighboring draw pile and water background, labelled 'HUD DETAIL'. Its crown must exactly match that column's card cost badge, blank token thumbnail and battle HUD.
Every item fully within canvas with breathing room. Do not add other variants. ALL proposed icons must be simple red crown-shaped silhouettes, NEVER the previous circular red medallion plus a tiny golden crown. Make the silhouette and numeral legibility the point of the image. This is a concept composition, not proof of actual game integration.
```
