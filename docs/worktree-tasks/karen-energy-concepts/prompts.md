# Imagegen prompts

工具：内置 image_gen（非 CLI）；背景：不透明炭灰；本轮为概念设计，不是可直接接入的分层资产。

## A / 星形发饰

```text
Use case: ui-mockup / stylized-concept.
Create ONE polished landscape game UI concept sheet, approximately 1536x1024, for Karen / Aijo Karen from Revue Starlight as a Slay the Spire 2 mod character.
This is a concept proposal for a pair of energy UI assets: the small card energy cost badge (native 74x74) and a matching larger combat HUD energy counter (native 128x128). NOT card illustrations, not character art.
Art style must be close to existing Slay the Spire 2 energy UI: bold dark outer ink outline, chunky hand-painted faceted surfaces, slightly imperfect paint texture, simple high contrast colored core, limited warm highlights. It should still look readable when scaled to native sizes. NOT photorealistic, NOT a glossy mobile gacha gem, no 3D bevel render, no fine filigree. Every asset viewed straight-on with no perspective. The existing game's classes have green angular, red octagonal, gold sun, pink hexagonal, and blue square energy frames. Give Karen an identifiable distinct silhouette and motif.
Karen palette is coral red #FB5458, deep burgundy #8B2A2D, warm ivory and restrained pale gold. Keep the number area spacious, mid-dark coral inside, and show large ivory serif numbers with thick dark burgundy outlines. Do not put an icon on top of the numbers. Energy values are a numeric overlay; no number designed into the material itself.
Presentation layout consistent throughout: charcoal neutral background #201e24, tasteful sparse labels, no scene or character in the background. Top title with concept letter and name. Left third: large blank card cost badge and another with '1', then a simple cropped red card upper-left corner showing that badge at a realistic small size. Right two thirds: largest matching combat orb centered with '3/3'; the counter must have the same icon identity as the small badge, with richer outer ornament. Bottom strip: one small inline text energy symbol without number, native-size 74px badge, native-size 128px combat orb, and a smaller depleted '0/3' state with dimmed core. Ensure the large hero counter, small cost badge and zero state all share exactly the same design. All elements fully within canvas, ample spacing. Labels only: concept title, 'CARD COST', 'COMBAT ENERGY', 'SMALL SIZE', 'EMPTY'. Do not print any long explanatory paragraph. No watermark.
Concept title verbatim: 'A — STAR HAIRCLIP'.
Primary request: Interpret Karen's iconic golden two-star hairclip as a functional energy badge. Main frame is a strong FOUR-POINT STAR of pale champagne gold, with slightly asymmetric long top and left arms; a second tiny matching four-point star attached at its lower-right edge and a short hairpin bar subtly extending left behind the main frame. Wide central circular coral-red enamel energy well leaves plenty of space for the number. Star gold is a broad simple frame around this red well, never crossing its middle. Dark charcoal ink outline makes the pale gold silhouette legible. Larger combat orb has two or three warm star glints and a subtle rotating-looking circular red energy band inside; tiny companion star is a secondary accent. Maximum two star shapes total. Small badge simplifies the same two-star silhouette and keeps readable digits. It should feel warm, personal, iconic to Karen, energetic and not royal. Golden star frame must not read as the Regent's orange multi-point sun; exactly four primary arms, with much more coral-red center and pale-gold frame.
```
